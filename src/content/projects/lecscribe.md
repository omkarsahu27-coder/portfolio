### The Problem
Manually taking notes during fast-paced lectures often leads to missed information.

### The Solution
I built an automated pipeline using local AI models to ensure data privacy and low latency. 

* **Transcription:** Handled by Faster-Whisper.
* **Summarization:** Structured using Llama 3 via Ollama.
